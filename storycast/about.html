About & Access

Why StoryCast exists

Young people make up most of Rwanda's population, and many of them are already telling powerful stories online. StoryCast brings some of those stories together and makes them easier for everyone to follow.

Our mission

A story is only shared when everyone can follow it, whether they listen, watch or read.

We pick short videos where young Rwandans talk about what they are building, from businesses to disability-rights networks. We embed each one from its original source, credit the creator, and write a full timestamped transcript.

Video can shut people out. A Deaf viewer may miss what is said, and a slow connection may not load the video at all. A transcript fixes both, and so does a site that works with keyboards and screen readers.

Accessibility features

StoryCast aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. Here is what that means in practice.

  • Captions

    Our intro video has its own WebVTT captions, loaded with the <track> element and on by default. Story videos are embedded with YouTube captions switched on.

  • Transcripts

    Every story and our audio intro have a full transcript on the page. Click any timestamp to jump the player to that moment, or download a .txt copy.

  • Keyboard navigation

    A skip link comes first on every page. Menus, players, transcript controls and cards all work with Tab, Enter, Space and Escape.

  • Visible focus

    Every interactive element shows a thick blue focus outline, so you always know where you are on the page.

  • High contrast

    All text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Body text is 15.7:1 on the paper background; the lowest pairing used is 5.6:1.

  • Reduced motion

    If your device asks for reduced motion, StoryCast turns off its hover animations and smooth scrolling.
